Global Privacy Policy

Effective Date: January 1, 2026 • Last Revised: October 2026 • Version 4.1

1. Scope and Identity of Controller

This Privacy Notice governs personal data processed by ShopFlow Global Technologies Inc. ("ShopFlow", "we", "us") in connection with our edge dispatch infrastructure, application programming interfaces (APIs), and customer portal accessible via our registered host domains.

2. Data Collection and Processing Grounds

We act primarily as a Data Processor on behalf of our enterprise merchant subscribers, processing retail order transaction metadata strictly to execute order fulfillment, payment clearing, and inventory sync requests. We collect:

  • Technical Metadata: IP addresses, TLS session cipher configurations, edge gateway round-trip latency, and protocol headers necessary for traffic routing and DDoS prevention.
  • Transaction Records: Pseudonymized order identifiers, shipping country codes, and transaction token hashes. Full cardholder numbers are never received or retained by ShopFlow servers.
  • Portal Authentication Logs: FIDO2 credentials, session tokens, and administrative audit trails.

3. Data Subject Rights (GDPR & CCPA)

Pursuant to European General Data Protection Regulation (Regulation EU 2016/679) and applicable international statutes, individuals have the following enforceable rights:

  • Right of Access: You may request verification of whether personal records concerning you are subject to processing.
  • Right to Rectification: Inaccurate data points will be corrected upon authenticated notice.
  • Right to Erasure ("Right to be Forgotten"): Personal identifier traces can be expunged from production partitions subject to statutory regulatory ledger hold periods.
  • Right to Data Portability: Transmission of stored metadata in structured, machine-readable JSON formats.
